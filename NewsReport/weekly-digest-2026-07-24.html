Richmond from The Investor here, dropping into your inbox with the latest pulse from the private capital markets. What a week it's been! If you blinked, you might've missed another billion-dollar deal, because this week was absolutely buzzing, especially with game-changing AI plays.

The diversity of innovation, especially in deep tech and health, really stood out.

Let's dive into the details that matter to your portfolio.

Top Investor Spotlight: Kirsten Green, Forerunner Ventures

This week, our spotlight shines on Kirsten Green, the visionary founder of Forerunner Ventures. A true titan in consumer and commerce technology investments, Kirsten's firm has a remarkable track record of identifying and nurturing disruptive startups. This week, Forerunner's influence was palpable, leading significant rounds for Natural, an innovative AI-native payments platform, and playing a key role in making Fora, an AI-powered travel agency, a unicorn. Her consistent eye for transformative potential, especially in leveraging AI to redefine market categories, continues to make her a force in the venture landscape.

Major Deals of the Week

Atoms: $1.7 Billion Undisclosed Round

Investors: a16z, Uber

Travis Kalanick's latest venture, Atoms, a robotics company leveraging industrial AI to modernize various industries, secured a staggering $1.7 billion. This massive funding round signals immense investor confidence in its AI-driven robotics solutions to revolutionize logistics and deep tech sectors.

IM8: $1 Billion from General Catalyst's CVF fund

Investors: General Catalyst (Customer Value Fund)

David Beckham's health drink startup, IM8, focusing on longevity vitamin drinks, raised an exceptional $1 billion. Notably, this funding came from General Catalyst's unique Customer Value Fund, which provides capital without taking equity, highlighting an innovative financing approach for established brands.

Anthropic: $500 Million Series D

Investors: Menlo Ventures

A prominent AI powerhouse, Anthropic, secured $500 million in a Series D round. This substantial investment, led by Menlo Ventures, is set to propel its ongoing research and development in large language models and other cutting-edge artificial intelligence technologies.

Chai Discovery: $400 Million Series C

Investors: Index Ventures, Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia Capital, Dimension, and many more.

Biotech startup Chai Discovery raised $400 million in Series C funding at a $3.8 billion valuation. This capital will accelerate its AI-driven molecular design and pre-clinical drug discovery efforts, with its technology already being adopted by major pharmaceutical companies like Eli Lilly and Pfizer.

Freenome: $310 Million IPO (including PIPE)

Investors: Perceptive Advisors, RA Capital, and public investors.

Freenome, a company pioneering early cancer detection through blood tests and AI, went public via a business combination, raising approximately $310 million. This significant step aims to make its life-saving technology for early-stage cancer diagnostics widely available.
