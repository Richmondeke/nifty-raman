Weekly private capital & events briefing

Hey there,

Richmond from The Investor here, bringing you the lowdown on private capital from the past week. It's been a fascinating few days, with some really big plays and clear trends emerging. While AI continues to dominate headlines and investment dollars – seriously, it feels like everyone's buzzing about it – we're also seeing some serious muscle flexed in deep tech, industrial automation, and even a solid bet on eSports.

What's clear is that investors aren't just chasing the shiny new AI thing; they're looking for where AI can truly transform "real economy" sectors like manufacturing, construction, and even legal services. And let's not forget the foundational infrastructure needed to power all this tech. Grab a coffee, let's dive into the deals.

Top Investor of the Week

Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)

Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) is a highly influential venture capital firm that backs bold entrepreneurs building the future through technology. Known for its strong presence in software, AI, fintech, crypto, and healthcare, a16z provides not just capital but also operational expertise and a powerful network to its portfolio companies.

This week, their strategic investments across legal tech AI (Stilta), wealth management AI (Moment), and industrial AI (Ciridae) underscore their continued belief in AI's transformative potential across diverse sectors of the "real economy." They are truly at the forefront of identifying and scaling disruptive technologies.

Major Deals of the Week

Armada: $230 Million Series B

This San Francisco-based edge computing platform snagged an oversubscribed Series B at a whopping $2 billion pre-money valuation. They're building modular data centers and megawatt-scale AI infrastructure, tackling the massive demand for remote edge-GPU compute in places without traditional cloud access.

Investors: Overmatch, BlackRock, 8090 Industries, Johnson Controls, NightDragon, Mitsui & Co., Singtel Innov8.

Nourish: $100 Million Series C

A virtual metabolic health clinic, Nourish closed a substantial Series C to expand its network of registered dietitians and enhance its AI-powered health platforms. They connect patients with dietitians for insurance-covered metabolic healthcare, including GLP-1 therapy support.

Investors: Menlo Ventures, Thrive Capital, Index Ventures, J.P. Morgan Growth Equity Partners, Y Combinator, Atomico.

Moment: $78 Million Series C

This AI-native operating system for wealth and investment management secured a hefty Series C. Their platform brings together trading, portfolio management, compliance, and reporting into one AI-driven system, now managing over $10 trillion in client assets.

Investors: Index Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Avra, existing early-stage investors.

Boston Metal: $75 Million Series C

A green steel startup, Boston Metal raised Series C funding to scale its Molten Oxide Electrolysis (MOE) platform. This tech recovers high-value metals from waste streams, strengthens supply chains, and aims to decarbonize metals production – big stuff for cleantech.

Investors: Tata Steel Limited, existing investors.
