Weekly private capital & events briefing

Hey Investor,

Richmond here from The Investor, kicking off your weekly briefing with a look back at an absolutely buzzing week in private capital! If there's one takeaway, it's that AI continues to be the undisputed heavyweight champion, pulling in colossal rounds across enterprise solutions, infrastructure, and specialized hardware.

We also saw some truly impressive deep tech action in space and a significant spotlight on African growth markets. The valuations are still eye-popping, showing that conviction in transformative tech remains incredibly strong. Let's dive into the details!

Top Investor of the Week: SoftBank

This week, our hats are off to SoftBank, who once again demonstrated their appetite for massive, growth-stage deals by co-leading the $2 billion round for Thrive Holdings. With their consistent participation alongside other giants like D1 Capital Partners and Altimeter Capital, SoftBank continues to be a pivotal force in scaling ambitious AI-driven enterprises. Their strategic bets underline a clear conviction in companies leveraging AI to revolutionize traditional sectors.

Major Deals of the Week

Thrive Holdings: $2 Billion at $12 Billion Valuation

Investors: SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, Altimeter Capital

This OpenAI-backed holding company is all about bringing AI solutions to the enterprise, often by acquiring traditional service businesses and embedding AI into their operations. This colossal round underscores the massive potential investors see in AI-driven efficiency for legacy industries.

ICEYE: €1 Billion Series F at over €10 Billion Valuation

Investors: EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund, General Atlantic, Seraphim Space, OTB Ventures

This Finnish powerhouse provides sovereign intelligence solutions from space, operating one of the world's largest SAR satellite constellations. This massive round, including €450 million in primary investment, fuels their expansion in critical deep tech and earth observation.

Groq: $350 Million Series A at $3.5 Billion Valuation

Investors: Disruptive (lead), NVIDIA

Groq is building leading AI compute infrastructure, specializing in AI inference. With NVIDIA's backing, this significant Series A signals a strong belief in their hardware and cloud platform to accelerate the next generation of AI applications.

OLIX: $312 Million Series B at $3.3 Billion Valuation

Investors: Fundomo (lead), Arm, Hudson River Trading, Reed Hastings

This London-based AI chip startup is taking on the giants by developing specialized silicon for frontier AI inference, using photonic interconnects for incredibly fast and efficient data movement. A serious player in the deep tech hardware race.

Rillet: $100 Million Series C at $1 Billion Valuation

Investors: Iconiq

Rillet, an AI-native account startup, officially hit unicorn status this week. Doubling its ARR in just three months, the company is demonstrating explosive growth in the enterprise AI software sector, showing how quickly new AI paradigms are being adopted.

Deal Directory: Notable & Early Stage

  • Terra Industries: $52 Million Seed Round. Africa's largest seed round for a Nigerian defense-technology company focused on autonomous systems. Investors include 8VC, Silent Ventures.
  • Jumia: $50 Million Equity Raise. African e-commerce giant securing funds from IFC and Axian to strengthen its balance sheet and push towards profitability.
  • Bruno: $3 Million Seed Round. Open-source, local-first, Git-native API Client, backed by Silverton Partners.
  • Vendo: Y Combinator Summer 2026 Batch. Open-source platform enabling custom micro-apps and features directly on existing software.
  • Speko: YC S26 Seed Funding. Developing an 'OpenRouter for Voice AI,' focusing on managing and routing various voice AI models.
