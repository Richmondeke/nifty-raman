Your Private Capital & Events Roundup

Hey there, it's Richmond from The Investor!

What a week it's been in the world of private capital. If you blink, you might miss a multi-billion dollar deal, especially when it comes to AI and healthtech. This past week saw some truly massive checks being written, cementing artificial intelligence as the undisputed champion of investor interest, with eye-watering valuations becoming the new norm across the board. Health and longevity also had a banner week, proving that investors are keen to back companies helping us live longer, healthier lives – sometimes even with a celebrity endorsement or two. So grab your preferred beverage, and let's dive into the week's biggest moves.

Top Investor Spotlight: General Catalyst

This week, a firm that truly stood out was General Catalyst. Not content with traditional equity investments, their innovative Customer Value Fund made headlines by injecting a whopping $1 billion into David Beckham's longevity drink startup, IM8. This "non-equity" approach signals a strategic flexibility that's catching eyes across the market, offering capital for growth without diluting founders' stakes. Beyond IM8, General Catalyst was also a key player in the $700 million Series C for Neko Health (co-founded by Spotify's Daniel Ek) and participated in the $400 million Series C for AI drug discovery firm Chai Discovery, further solidifying their commitment to transformative healthtech and cutting-edge AI. Their diverse and impactful plays this week highlight a firm that's thinking outside the box and backing big bets in high-growth sectors.

The Week's Blockbusters: Multi-Billion Dollar Plays

Databricks: $5 Billion

Valuation: $188 Billion

Investors: Coatue

The data and AI powerhouse secured a gargantuan $5 billion investment, cementing its position as a leader in data analytics and AI. This colossal funding round underscores immense investor confidence in its ability to drive future innovation and expansion in the enterprise software space.

DeepSeek: $7 Billion

Valuation: $52 Billion

Investors: Liang Wenfeng, Tencent, CATL

This Chinese artificial intelligence company burst onto the scene with its first external funding round, raising an incredible $7 billion. The investment solidifies DeepSeek's global standing in advanced AI model development, backed by significant players including Tencent.

Fireworks: $1.505 Billion Series D

Valuation: $17.5 Billion

Investors: Atreides Management, Index Ventures, TCV, NVIDIA, and others.

Providing specialized intelligence infrastructure for building and serving custom AI models, Fireworks crossed the $1 billion annualized revenue mark and picked up over $1.5 billion in new funding, highlighting strong demand for enterprise AI solutions.

IM8 (David Beckham's Health Drink): $1 Billion

Investors: General Catalyst (Customer Value Fund)

David Beckham's longevity vitamin drink startup secured a monumental $1 billion from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund. This unique non-equity investment signals a huge vote of confidence in celebrity-backed ventures within the booming health and wellness sector.

Neko Health: $700 Million Series C

Investors: Lightspeed VP, O.G. VP, Atomico, General Catalyst, Mark Zuckerberg & Priscilla Chan, Tim Ferriss, Maria Sharapova, will.i.am, Thierry Henry, Jimmy Iovine, and many other prominent figures.

Co-founded by Spotify's Daniel Ek, this healthtech company offers non-invasive preventive health assessments. This massive Series C, backed by a roster of top-tier VCs and influential HNWIs, fuels its planned US launch and accelerates research into early disease detection.

Chai Discovery: $400 Million Series C

Valuation: $3.8 Billion

Investors: Index Ventures (lead), Kleiner Perkins, Sequoia Capital, Dimension, Bain Capital Ventures, OpenAI, and many others.

This AI drug discovery startup tripled its valuation to $3.8 billion with a $400 million Series C. Chai Discovery leverages advanced AI models to predict and reprogram biochemical interactions, accelerating drug development in partnership with major pharmaceutical companies.

Deal Directory: Early Stage & Emerging Tech

  • Norm (AI Law Startup): $120 Million Series C

    Unicorn Status: $1.2 Billion Valuation. Investors: Khosla Ventures (lead), Blackstone, Bain Capital Ventures. Developing AI agents for legal and regulatory work, achieving unicorn status by embedding law into AI agents.

  • Gradium (AI Voice Startup): $100 Million Seed

    Investors: NVIDIA, FirstMark Capital, Eric Schmidt, Xavier Niel. Paris-based startup specializing in ultra-low-latency voice AI models and infrastructure for developers, expanding to the Bay Area.

  • Nous Research (AI Agents): $75 Million

    Valuation: $1.5 Billion. Investors: Robot Ventures (lead), Union Square Ventures (USV). The developer of Hermes agents is strengthening its position in the AI agent landscape with significant new funding.

  • Fora (AI-Powered Travel Agency): $60 Million Series D

    Unicorn Status: $1 Billion Valuation. Investors: Forerunner, Tactile Ventures. This AI-driven personalized and efficient travel planning service has achieved unicorn status, highlighting growth in AI travel solutions.

  • Helios Towers (Telecom Infra): $29 Million

    Investors: Standard Bank. Operating across Africa, this investment supports their vital work in building and managing tower networks, enhancing regional connectivity.

  • Traceforce (AI Security Monitoring): Undisclosed Seed (YC S26)

    Investors: Y Combinator. This YC S26 startup focuses on providing company-wide security monitoring for AI applications, giving businesses much-needed visibility and control over AI tools used by employees.
