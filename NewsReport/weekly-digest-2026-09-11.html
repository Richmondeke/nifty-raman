Weekly Roundup

September 11, 2026 Edition

Private Capital & Events Briefing

What a week, folks! Richmond here from The Investor, and if you blinked, you probably missed another billion-dollar valuation flying by. This past week felt like an AI gold rush, with companies building the very foundations of the AI revolution attracting eye-watering sums. From massive data centers powering the next generation of models to sophisticated AI agents automating complex tasks, the smart money is pouring into making AI bigger, faster, and smarter. But it wasn't just about silicon and software; we also saw some interesting plays in clean energy, manufacturing, and even professional sports, showing that innovation is truly hitting every field. Grab a coffee, because we've got a lot to unpack!

Top Investor / Dealmaker of the Week

Craig Shapiro, Collaborative Fund

This week, we're tipping our hat to Craig Shapiro and the Collaborative Fund for their shrewd and unconventional investment in D.C. United and Audi Field. While many VCs are caught in the AI frenzy, Shapiro's move into professional sports ownership isn't just about a team; it’s a brilliant strategy to create a unique showcase and distribution platform for Collaborative Fund's portfolio startups. This foresight transforms a traditional asset into a living lab and marketing channel, demonstrating a truly innovative approach to value creation beyond typical tech plays. It's a reminder that sometimes the most impactful moves are made outside the obvious lanes.

Major Deals of the Week

Crusoe: $3 Billion Series F at $30 Billion Valuation

This Denver-based powerhouse, initially known for crypto mining, has successfully pivoted to become a leading AI infrastructure and data center provider. Securing a colossal $3 billion in Series F funding, Crusoe now supplies GPU infrastructure to giants like OpenAI, Microsoft, and Meta, cementing its position in the rapidly expanding AI compute market.

Key Investors: Atreides Management, Valor Equity Partners, Mubadala Capital.

Cognition (Devin): $2 Billion Series E at $48 Billion Valuation

The company behind Devin, the autonomous coding agent, raised a staggering $2 billion, pushing its valuation to $48 billion. This investment underscores the massive demand for AI agents capable of handling complex software development tasks and automating engineering workflows.

Key Investors: Andreessen Horowitz, Accel, Founders Fund, General Catalyst, NVIDIA, and many more.

Mistral: €3 Billion Series D at €21 Billion Valuation

European AI champion Mistral secured a monumental €3 billion funding round, spearheaded by Samsung. This investment solidifies Mistral's growing influence and strategic position in the fiercely competitive large language model and deep learning market.

Key Investors: Samsung (lead).

Harvey: $550 Million at $15.6 Billion Valuation

The legal AI startup Harvey raised a substantial $550 million round, reaching a $15.6 billion valuation. This capital will accelerate its mission to help law firms and legal teams build and manage their own intelligence at scale, advancing AI-powered legal solutions.

Key Investors: Diffusion, Lightspeed Venture Partners (co-leads), Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz (a16z).

Miro: Acquired for $1.36 Billion by Bending Spoons

The widely popular online collaborative whiteboard platform, Miro, is being acquired by Bending Spoons for approximately $1.36 billion. This significant M&A move highlights the continued value and consolidation in the collaboration software market.

Acquirer: Bending Spoons.
