October 02, 2026 Edition

Weekly private capital & events briefing

Hi there, Richmond from The Investor

What a week it's been! If you blinked, you might have missed a few billion-dollar rounds. This past week clearly showed that AI isn't just a buzzword; it's a full-blown infrastructure race and a new frontier for almost every sector. We're seeing massive capital pouring into building the foundational layers for AI, from data centers to specialized chips, alongside significant investments in practical applications like healthtech and enterprise automation.

It's a strong signal that investors are betting big on companies that can either power the AI revolution or leverage it to transform traditional industries. And hey, even venture capital firms themselves are raising monster funds specifically for AI. So, grab a coffee, and let's dive into the deals that shaped the week!

Top Investor of the Week: Bessemer Venture Partners

This week, our hats are off to **Bessemer Venture Partners**. They not only co-led a hefty $350 million Series F round for EliseAI, doubling its valuation to $4 billion, but they also announced a staggering **$5.75 billion** additional capital raise specifically for AI investments. That's a serious commitment to the future of artificial intelligence!

Bessemer's strategic focus on AI-native companies, driven by what they describe as unprecedented growth rates, positions them as a key player in shaping the next generation of tech. Their dual approach of backing high-growth AI companies and deploying massive funds into the sector makes them a standout dealmaker this week.

Major Deals of the Week

Nscale: $3.36 Billion Convertible Financing

Investors: Third Point, Nvidia, Apollo, Citadel, Hudson Bay Capital, Abu Dhabi Investment Council, and more.

This British AI neocloud company is fueling its massive AI data center buildout with convertible financing ahead of an anticipated U.S. IPO. It's clear the AI infrastructure race is on, and Nscale is positioning itself at the forefront of this capital-intensive expansion.

Instinct: $1 Billion Series C

Investors: Sequoia Capital, Benchmark Capital, Coatue.

Valued at $10 billion, Instinct is developing a personal AI agent designed to handle complex everyday tasks autonomously. This mega-round underscores the growing belief in the power of AI agents to simplify our lives.

Neko Health: $700 Million Series C

Investors: Lightspeed Venture Partners, O.G. Venture Partners, Atomico, Mark Zuckerberg, Priscilla Chan, Maria Sharapova, will.i.am, Tim Ferriss, OpenAI, and more.

Co-founded by Spotify's Daniel Ek, Neko Health is expanding its preventative healthcare platform into the U.S. with non-invasive diagnostic scans. A huge vote of confidence in proactive, tech-driven health solutions.

EliseAI: $350 Million Series F

Investors: Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Bessemer Venture Partners, Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan, Sapphire Ventures, Navitas Capital.

Doubling its valuation to $4 billion, EliseAI is automating complex housing and healthcare systems with its enterprise AI solutions. It's a testament to the strong demand for AI in streamlining operational workflows.

Efficient Computer: $97 Million Series B

Investors: TQ Ventures, Eclipse, Union Square Ventures, Giant Ventures, Toyota Ventures, and more.

This Pittsburgh-based startup is developing energy-efficient processor architectures for AI, aiming for significant reductions in energy consumption for data centers. Deep tech making waves in sustainable computing.
