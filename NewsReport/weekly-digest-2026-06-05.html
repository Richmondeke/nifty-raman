Weekly Roundup

June 05, 2026

Weekly private capital & events briefing

Hey there, Richmond from The Investor here. What a whirlwind week in the private capital markets! If you've been tracking things, you'll know that Space and AI continued their fierce ascent, commanding some serious capital. We saw a monster IPO planned from a certain rocket company and huge rounds for generative AI powerhouses. But it wasn't just the usual suspects; electric mobility in Africa is absolutely buzzing, and climate tech is carving out its own significant niche. It's a clear signal that innovation is still firing on all cylinders, attracting big bets from across the spectrum.

Top Investor of the Week

Mike Schroepfer (Gigascale Capital)

This week, a big shout-out goes to **Mike Schroepfer**, the visionary former CTO of Meta. Mike's new venture, Gigascale Capital, just closed its first institutional climate fund at a impressive $250 million. His firm is zeroing in on early-stage founders who are rebuilding the physical economy, with a sharp focus on energy, grid infrastructure, critical minerals, and industrial technologies. It’s all about backing companies that deliver cheaper, faster, and more reliable products, with climate impact being a natural, positive ripple effect. A truly impactful move for the planet and portfolios!

Major Deals of the Week

SpaceX: $75 Billion IPO

The aerospace giant is eyeing a massive IPO, targeting $75 billion at $135 per share, aiming for a $1.75 trillion valuation. Leading in reusable rocket tech and satellite internet, this move will further fuel their ambitious space exploration and connectivity goals.

Investors: Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, BofA Securities, Citigroup, J.P.Morgan.

Anthropic: $65 Billion Series H

The AI firm specializing in large language models like Claude secured an enormous Series H, valuing them at $965 billion. This capital will turbocharge their safety research, expand compute capacity, and scale products, solidifying their spot as an AI behemoth.

Investors: Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia Capital, Capital Group, and many more industry heavyweights.

Impulse Space: $500 Million Series D

Founded by former SpaceX propulsion chief Tom Mueller, Impulse Space is rocketing ahead with its in-space mobility solutions. This funding will scale production of orbital transfer vehicles and expand the team, providing crucial 'last-mile' delivery in the booming space economy.

Investors: 137 Ventures, BANNER VC (co-led); Founders Fund, Lux Capital, Linse Capital.

Suno: Over $400 Million Series D

Suno is hitting all the right notes with its AI music generation platform, empowering users to create custom music from text prompts. This significant funding will accelerate hiring, new product development, and expand its reach for artists and creators.

Investors: Bond Capital (lead); IVP, Forerunner, Union Square Ventures, Alkeon, Quiet, Matrix, Lightspeed, Menlo Ventures, Schroders Capital.

Spiro: $215 Million Equity Funding

This African electric mobility company is supercharging its expansion with substantial equity funding. Spiro is building crucial battery-swapping infrastructure for motorcycles across Africa, aiming to deepen its presence in existing markets and break into new ones like Ethiopia.

Investors: Impact Fund Denmark, Equitane.
