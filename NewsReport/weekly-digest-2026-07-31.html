Weekly private capital & events briefing

Hey everyone, Richmond here from The Investor!

What a week it's been in the world of private capital! If there's one thing that truly dominated our headlines, it's the sheer force of AI. From massive multi-billion dollar rounds for established players to significant seeds for cutting-edge infrastructure and content detection, artificial intelligence continues to draw enormous interest and capital. We're seeing big bets on deep tech and infrastructure too, with Elon Musk's tunneling ambitions and Travis Kalanick's robotics venture securing colossal investments. It's clear that investors are still leaning into transformative technologies that promise to reshape industries from the ground up.

Let's dive into the details of who's raising what and who's making waves.

Top Investor of the Week: Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)

This week, the spotlight shines brightly on Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), once again demonstrating their commanding presence in the venture landscape. Not only did they lead Travis Kalanick's robotics company Atoms in a whopping $1.7 billion round, but they also co-led Anduril's $5 billion Series H and backed early-stage construction tech via a16z Speedrun. Their consistent pursuit of bold, foundational technology and their backing of visionary founders truly set them apart. It's clear that when it comes to shaping the future, a16z is right there at the forefront, pouring significant capital into the companies they believe will make the biggest impact.

Major Deals of the Week

Moonshot AI: $3.5 Billion Series F

Investors: National Artificial Intelligence Industry Investment Fund, Alibaba, Tencent, HongShan, IDG Capital, Gaorong Capital

This Chinese AI powerhouse, known for its Kimi chatbot, secured a massive Series F at a $35 billion valuation. The substantial investment will fuel further development of its advanced artificial intelligence capabilities and large language models, solidifying its position in the global AI race.

The Boring Company: Reportedly raising $4 Billion

Investors: Undisclosed

Elon Musk's ambitious tunneling startup is reportedly in talks for a significant new funding round, which would value the company at $20 billion. The capital aims to advance its mission to alleviate urban congestion through high-speed underground transportation systems.

Anduril: $5 Billion Series H

Investors: Thrive Capital (lead), Andreessen Horowitz (lead)

The defense technology giant secured a massive Series H, valuing the company at $61 billion. This funding will be crucial for scaling its advanced defense systems, further investing in manufacturing, R&D, and infrastructure leveraging AI and autonomous technologies.

Atoms: $1.7 Billion

Investors: a16z (lead), Uber, Bain Capital Ventures, Fifth Wall, A*, Chemistry, K5 Global, Abstract, SV Angel, Alpha Square Group

Travis Kalanick's robotics company landed a substantial investment to modernize various global sectors using industrial AI. Atoms develops integrated systems of AI, software, sensors, and specialized robotics to automate physical operations, with Uber also participating.

Anthropic: $500 Million Series D

Investors: Menlo Ventures (lead)

This leading AI company, which saw unprecedented growth, secured a significant Series D. Led by Menlo Ventures, this funding will further advance Anthropic's development of sophisticated AI models, including its well-known large language models.

Deal Directory: Early Stage & Noteworthy

Big Seeds & Series A Rounds

  • Prentis: In talks to raise $100 Million at $1 Billion valuation for new AI lab co-founded by Reid Hoffman and Mark Pincus, developing AI agents for office workflows.
  • Natural: $30 Million Series A to build payment infrastructure for AI agents, challenging Stripe. (Lead: Forerunner Ventures)
  • Infinity: $15 Million Seed at a $100 Million valuation for AI inference infrastructure. (Lead: Touring Capital)
  • Bluecore Energy: $10 Million Pre-seed for maritime nuclear energy, developing portable reactors on barges. (Lead: Slauson & Co.)
  • Pangram: $9 Million to enhance AI content detection technology to identify AI-generated text and images. (Investors: Menlo Ventures, Haystack, ScOp, Script Capital, Cadenza)

Early Stage & Niche Innovators

  • Imagi: $4.5 Million Seed for an edtech platform teaching 'vibe code' to make learning engaging. (Investors: Brighteye Ventures, Day One Capital, Will.i.am)
  • Cascade: $3.5 Million Seed for a platform helping construction firms secure new projects. (Investors: a16z Speedrun, Ada Ventures, Snowball VC)
  • Fincart: $2.8 Million Seed for an Egyptian AI-powered commerce platform for e-commerce merchants. (Co-leads: Launch Africa, Antler MENAP)
  • European Tech Network (ETN): $1.6 Million Seed for a London-based tech media platform focusing on European startups. (Investors: Powerhouse Capital, Axel Springer SE, LadBible)
  • Rise Reforming: $650 Thousand Pre-seed for YC cleantech converting waste gases into valuable chemicals. (Investors: Y Combinator, 776 Foundation, U.S. Dept. of Energy, etc.)

Other Notable Movements

  • Leatherback: Acquisition by Zedcrest Group. African cross-border payments startup acquired to bolster Zedcrest's fintech presence.
  • Situational Awareness: Leopold Aschenbrenner's AI-focused hedge fund is seeking additional capital following recent losses in AI-related stocks.
