Hey folks,

Richmond here from The Investor, bringing you the week's rundown. If you thought AI was just a buzzword, this week's deals should set you straight. We've seen some absolutely massive raises, clearly indicating that investors are doubling down on everything from foundational models to specialized AI applications.

Beyond the AI boom, there's continued strong interest in deep tech like fusion energy and robust solutions for e-commerce logistics. It's clear that while AI is stealing headlines, smart money is also looking at the next generation of critical infrastructure and sustainability. Let's dive in!

Top Dealmaker of the Week

Founders Fund

This week, Founders Fund stood out with their continued strong backing of groundbreaking companies. They're not just placing bets; they're shaping the future, showing up in the colossal Anthropic Series G and the strategic Series F for Stord. Their consistent presence in both cutting-edge AI and critical infrastructure demonstrates a clear vision for where the market is headed. They're definitely a firm to watch for defining the next era of innovation.

Major Deals of the Week

Anthropic: $30 Billion Series G

Investors: GIC, Coatue, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia, Sequoia Capital, Altimeter Capital, Founders Fund, among others.

Anthropic is reportedly closing a staggering $30 billion funding round, pushing its valuation past $900 billion. This monumental investment, with significant contributions from tech giants like Google and Microsoft, solidifies its position at the forefront of generative AI research and development.

Cognition: $1 Billion Series D

Investors: Lux Capital, General Catalyst, Founders Fund, 8VC, Elad Gil, Bain Capital Ventures, Vitruvian, and more.

The AI coding startup behind Devin, the first AI software engineer, raised a hefty $1 billion at a $26 billion valuation. This massive round highlights the intense demand for autonomous coding tools and the rapid enterprise adoption of their end-to-end code generation capabilities.

Stord: $250 Million Series F

Investors: Strike Capital, Kleiner Perkins, Founders Fund, Franklin Templeton, Baillie Gifford, Bond, Lux.

Positioning itself as a strong competitor to Amazon's fulfillment, Stord secured $250 million at a $3 billion valuation. This funding will fuel the development of its "physical intelligence layer," enhancing e-commerce logistics with robotics and next-gen AI.

OpenRouter: $113 Million Series B

Investors: CapitalG, NVentures, ServiceNow Ventures, MongoDB Ventures, Snowflake Ventures, Databricks Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz, Menlo Ventures.

OpenRouter, an exchange for AI models, saw a $113 million Series B round. Their platform provides unified infrastructure to manage and optimize inference across over 400 models, essential as enterprises increasingly deploy AI agents and multi-model strategies.

Thea Energy: $100 Million Series B

Investors: US Innovative Technology Fund, Emerald Technology Ventures, Hitachi Ventures, Lowercarbon Capital, Prelude Ventures, and more.

Focused on building scalable fusion power plants using stellarator-based systems, Thea Energy secured $100 million. This funding will boost their magnet manufacturing capacity and accelerate the path towards commercial deployment of fusion energy.
