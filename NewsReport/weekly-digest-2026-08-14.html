Weekly Roundup

August 14, 2026 Edition

Weekly private capital & events briefing

Hey there,

Richmond from The Investor here, bringing you the latest pulse from the private capital markets. What a week it's been! The big takeaway? AI isn't just a buzzword anymore; it's the bedrock for a new wave of deep tech, from enterprise solutions to cutting-edge hardware. We're seeing massive capital pour into companies building the literal infrastructure for the AI revolution.

Beyond AI, deep tech is thriving across the board – next-gen energy storage and advanced satellite constellations are drawing serious interest. Europe is also stepping up, with significant funds backing its own scale-ups. It's clear that investors are ready to back ambitious visions that tackle big, complex problems. Let's dive into the details!

Top Dealmaker of the Week: Scaleup Europe Fund (managed by EQT)

This week, the spotlight shines brightly on the Scaleup Europe Fund, managed by EQT. Making waves with its first significant backing, this fund co-led a staggering €1 billion Series F round for Finnish satellite company ICEYE, demonstrating Europe's strategic commitment to deep tech. Not stopping there, they also co-led a substantial $400 million Series C for Swedish AI coding startup Lovable. The Scaleup Europe Fund's aggressive moves signal a powerful push to foster and scale European innovation, proving that home-grown tech can compete globally with ambitious capital behind it.

Major Deals of the Week

$2 Billion Growth for Thrive Holdings

Investors: SoftBank, D1 Capital Partners, Altimeter Capital

OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings secured a massive $2 billion to accelerate enterprise AI solutions, reaching a $12 billion valuation. This funding underscores strong market confidence in their technology for bringing advanced AI to large businesses.

$1.1 Billion Seed & Series A for River AI

Investors: General Catalyst, AMP PBC, NVIDIA, AMD Ventures, Y Combinator, Temasek

Founded by an xAI co-founder, River AI raised $1.1 billion at a $5 billion valuation to build an open AI stack, focusing on user-owned and personalized AI models for developers and enterprises.

€1 Billion Series F for ICEYE

Investors: Scaleup Europe Fund (EQT), General Atlantic, Solidium, QIA, TCV, and others

The Finnish satellite company, specializing in SAR technology, closed a massive €1 billion Series F, including €450 million in primary capital. Valued over €10 billion, ICEYE will expand its sovereign intelligence capabilities for defense and commercial applications.

$700 Million Series C for Lumilens

Investors: Atreides Management, Bain Capital Ventures, Meritech Capital, Seligman Ventures, Spark Capital, and others

Lumilens, a silicon photonics startup, secured over $700 million at a $5.51 billion valuation. They are developing optical interconnect technology to replace traditional data center wires with light, crucial for advanced AI infrastructure.

$750 Million Growth for Form Energy

Investors: T. Rowe Price, GE Vernova, Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Prelude Ventures, Macquarie Capital, The Engine

The US-based energy storage startup raised $750 million to advance its innovative iron-air 'rust-powered' battery technology, designed for long-duration grid stability with renewable energy sources.

Deal Directory: Other Notable Rounds

Key Growth & Later Stage Rounds

  • Moove: $250 Million Series C for autonomous vehicle fleet management and robotaxi infrastructure.
  • Olix: $312 Million Series B at a $3.3B valuation, building specialized silicon with photonic interconnects for AI workloads.
  • Lovable: $400 Million Series C at a $13.3B valuation for its AI-powered software creation platform.
  • CodeRabbit: $143 Million Series C at a $1.5B valuation for AI-powered code reviews and agentic change management.
  • Convex.dev: $57 Million Series B for its reactive backend platform, supporting human and AI developers.
  • Jumia: $50 Million Equity Funding to strengthen the balance sheet of the prominent African e-commerce company.

Emerging Innovators (Seed & Series A/B)

  • WindBorne Systems: $37 Million Series B for AI-powered weather prediction using atmospheric sensing balloons.
  • Naïve: $28.5 Million Series A to develop infrastructure for AI agents to automate business administrative tasks.
  • June: $20 Million Preseed (Marc Benioff-backed) to simplify enterprise AI deployment with AI agents.
  • Preview: $12 Million Seed (Sequoia-led) for its production platform for AI video generation.
  • Bullet: Undisclosed Seed (YC S26) launched with a faster coding agent for developer productivity.
  • Discovered Materials: Seed (YC P26) using AI agents to discover new materials.
  • Keet: Seed (YC S24) building an app to create video courses on any topic.
