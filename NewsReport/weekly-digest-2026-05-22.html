Weekly Private Capital & Events Briefing

May 22, 2026 Edition

A Word From Richmond

Hey everyone, Richmond here from The Investor! What a week it's been in private capital. If there's one clear takeaway, it's that AI isn't just a buzzword anymore—it's actively reshaping pretty much every sector you can think of. From industrial operations and construction to finance and healthcare, smart money is pouring into companies using AI to solve real-world problems. We're seeing massive rounds for edge computing, green tech making strides in critical metals, and even a couple of cool plays in gamification and social media holding their own against the AI tide. It’s clear that innovation, especially when coupled with a sustainable edge, is still king. Let's dive into the details!

Top Investor Spotlight: Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)

This week, the prolific venture firm Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) clearly stood out, demonstrating its strategic commitment to the bleeding edge of technology. Their backing spanned critical areas, from leading Moment's $78M Series C for AI-native wealth management to participating in Ciridae's $20M seed round for industrial AI operating systems. They also notably supported Stilta's $10.5M seed to automate legal IP research. A16z's consistent focus on foundational AI, across diverse sectors, reaffirms their position as a bellwether for where smart capital is heading next.

Major Deals of the Week

Armada: $230 Million Series B

Investors: Overmatch, BlackRock, 8090 Industries, Johnson Controls, NightDragon, Mitsui & Co., Singtel Innov8

This San Francisco-based edge computing platform snagged an oversubscribed round to fuel its modular AI infrastructure. Armada is building megawatt-scale and ruggedized data centers to meet the massive demand for remote edge-GPU compute, especially in environments lacking traditional cloud connectivity.

Nourish: $100 Million Series C

Investors: Menlo Ventures, Thrive Capital, Index Ventures, J.P. Morgan Growth Equity Partners, Y Combinator, Atomico

The virtual metabolic health clinic is expanding its network of dietitians and enhancing its AI-powered health platforms. Nourish connects patients with insurance-covered metabolic healthcare, including lab integration and GLP-1 therapy support, positioning itself as a leader in digital metabolic health.

Moment: $78 Million Series C

Investors: Index Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Avra, existing early-stage investors

Moment is building an AI-native operating system for wealth and investment management. Their platform streamlines trading, portfolio management, compliance, and reporting, with client wealth firms now managing over $10 trillion in combined assets.

Boston Metal: $75 Million Series C

Investors: Tata Steel Limited, Climate Investment, existing investors

This green steel startup is scaling its Molten Oxide Electrolysis (MOE) platform to produce critical metals sustainably. The funding will accelerate commercial deployment, recovering high-value metals from waste streams and contributing to decarbonization and secure supply chains.

Dust: $40 Million Series B

Investors: Sequoia, Abstract, Snowflake, Datadog

Paris-based Dust is developing an enterprise AI platform for 'multiplayer AI,' fostering human-agent collaboration in shared workspaces. This funding will advance self-learning agents, enhance collaborative tools, and strengthen enterprise-grade governance systems.
