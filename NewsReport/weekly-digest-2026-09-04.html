Richmond here from The Investor, bringing you the week's rundown. It's been an absolute whirlwind, especially if you're keeping an eye on AI and, surprisingly, the bustling markets across Africa. Let's dive into the details, shall we?

Top Investor of the Week: a16z's "Machine Age" Fund

This week, the spotlight's on Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) with the announcement of their new **$1.1 billion 'Machine Age' fund**. This isn't just another tech fund; it's a strategic move signaling a deeper commitment to the *physical buildout* of AI. While many are focused on software and models, a16z is looking at the foundational hardware and infrastructure that will power the next generation of artificial intelligence. This fund's focus on accelerating the tangible aspects of AI—from advanced manufacturing to robotics components—shows a keen understanding of where the bottlenecks (and thus, the opportunities) lie in scaling AI for widespread impact. It's a significant vote of confidence in the deep tech sector and a clear indicator of where smart money is heading for long-term gains in the AI race.

Major Deals of the Week

Crusoe

$3 Billion at a $30 Billion Valuation

Investors: Atreides Management, Valor Equity Partners, Mubadala Capital

Crusoe, a standout in data center development and AI infrastructure, secured a massive $3 billion round, skyrocketing its valuation to $30 billion. With big names like Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI as customers, their recent $13 billion cloud contract with Jane Street underscores their crucial role in powering next-gen AI.

Lovable

$400 Million Series C at a $13.3 Billion Valuation

Investors: Menlo Ventures (lead), EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund (co-lead), Balderton Capital, Carmignac, Kaszek Ventures, LTS Growth, Tencent, World Innovation Lab, Regent, Accel, Antler, CapitalG, DST Global, Evantic Capital, HubSpot Ventures, Salesforce Ventures

The Swedish 'vibe-coding' darling Lovable nearly doubled its valuation in eight months, reaching $13.3 billion with a $400 million Series C. Their AI-powered platform lets anyone build applications with natural language, democratizing software creation and racking up over 60 million projects since late 2024.

AfterQuery

Undisclosed Round at a $3.2 Billion Valuation

Investors: Undisclosed

AfterQuery has become Y Combinator's fastest-ever unicorn, hitting a $3.2 billion valuation just five months post-Series A. This AI model-training startup provides human-curated datasets and reinforcement learning environments to industry titans like NVIDIA, Motif, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind.

General Intuition

In talks to raise at a $6 Billion Pre-Money Valuation

Investors: Valor Ventures, Point72 Ventures, Seven Seven Six

This AI startup is making waves by building a foundational model to train generalized AI agents that learn to navigate space and time. Their reported $6 billion pre-money valuation indicates significant investor confidence in their ambitious push into the robotics sector and deep tech innovation.
