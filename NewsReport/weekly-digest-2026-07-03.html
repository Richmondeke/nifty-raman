Weekly Roundup

July 03, 2026

Private Capital & Events Briefing

Hey folks, Richmond here from The Investor! What a week it's been in the world of private capital. If there's one takeaway, it's this: AI is not just a buzzword; it's the engine driving innovation and investment across the board. From stress-testing AI agents to building entire software factories with them, and even personal AI assistants, the smart money is flowing heavily into intelligent automation. We also saw some serious dry powder being raised by top-tier VCs, signaling a continued appetite for bold bets. Let's dive into the details!

Top Investor of the Week: Menlo Ventures

This week, we're tipping our hats to Menlo Ventures. They've just closed a whopping $3 billion fund, a testament to their sharp eye for disruptive tech. Menlo made headlines back in 2024 for a bold $750 million investment in Anthropic, which at the time was a significant move. That bet clearly paid off, solidifying their reputation as a premier AI investor. Their continued success shows the power of conviction and strategic foresight in a rapidly evolving market.

Major Deals of the Week

8090: $135 Million Series A

Investors: Salesforce Ventures (lead), WNDR, Craft Ventures, The Production Board, LAUNCH, and various angels.

Chamath Palihapitiya's latest venture, 8090, is an AI-native software factory developing a "Software Factory" platform. This platform uses AI agents to help companies build and modify enterprise software, aiming to democratize access to intelligence and expertise in software development. A massive Series A shows strong confidence in their vision to revolutionize enterprise software.

Warp: $60 Million Series B

Investors: Battery Ventures, Peak XV, Sound Ventures, Y Combinator, and notable angels including Tobi Lütke.

Warp is making waves in Human Capital Management (HCM) with its AI-native platform. It automates critical HR, payroll, IT, and tax compliance tasks, leveraging AI agents to manage complex workflows across various tax jurisdictions. They're positioning themselves as a serious challenger to established HCM players.

Patronus AI: $50 Million Series B

Investors: Greenfield Partners (lead), Lightspeed Venture Partners, Notable Capital, Datadog, Samsung, Factorial Capital, Gokul Rajaram.

Founded by former Meta AI researchers, Patronus AI is building 'Digital World Models' – large-scale simulation environments to rigorously stress-test and train AI agents. This critical infrastructure addresses the high failure rates of AI agents, ensuring reliability before deployment and has seen 15x revenue growth.

Pocket: $11 Million (Undisclosed)

Investors: Accel, and others.

Pocket is carving out a niche in consumer tech with its personal AI assistant device. The startup is reportedly experiencing surging demand, indicating a strong public appetite for dedicated AI hardware that promises more seamless and integrated AI experiences.

Deal Directory: Early Stage & Fundraises
  • Parsewise: $500 Thousand Seed – Y Combinator-backed AI-driven data extraction for asset management, life sciences, and insurance.
  • Stabyl: $2.7 Million Preseed – Building critical foreign exchange (FX) infrastructure for Africa, led by Konga.
  • Valor Equity Partners: $2.5 Billion Fund VII – A significant new fund targeting investments across various sectors.
  • Seedcamp: $320 Million New Fund – Early-stage investor expanding its presence into the US market.
