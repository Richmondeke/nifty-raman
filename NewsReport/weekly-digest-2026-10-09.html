Weekly Investor Briefing

October 09, 2026 Edition

Private Capital & Events Roundup

Hey Investors, Richmond here!

What a week! If you've been following the private capital markets, you'll know AI continues to dominate headlines and deal flow. This past week saw some absolutely massive rounds, particularly in AI infrastructure and deep tech, with multi-billion dollar plays becoming almost... commonplace. We're seeing a clear trend of capital pouring into the foundational layers of AI, from quantum computing to specialized chips and physics simulations. Healthtech also grabbed serious attention, alongside a vibrant mix of fintech, robotics, and even some exciting developments out of Africa. It's a testament to the diverse opportunities still out there for the savvy investor.

Top Investor of the Week

Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)

This week, it's hard to ignore the pervasive presence of **Andreessen Horowitz** across multiple significant rounds. From co-leading EliseAI's whopping $350 million Series F at a $4 billion valuation to backing Nous Research's $90 million for open-source enterprise AI, and even leading Ghost's $11 million seed round for personal AI hardware, a16z is clearly bullish on the AI revolution at every stage. Their strategic investments underscore a deep conviction in the transformative power of AI across diverse applications, from enterprise automation to consumer-facing hardware. Their continued support for founders pushing the boundaries of technology makes them a standout dealmaker.

Major Deals of the Week

SpaceX: $40 Billion Debt Financing

Investors: Apollo Global Management (lead), Pimco (in talks), other banks & institutional investors.

Elon Musk's SpaceX is reportedly in talks to raise a staggering $40 billion in debt financing. This massive capital injection is aimed at funding the acquisition of Nvidia AI chips, highlighting SpaceX's deep commitment to building out significant AI infrastructure for its satellite internet and other AI-intensive projects. This isn't just about space anymore; it's about the data and intelligence powering it.

DeepSeek: At least $12 Billion Growth Round

Investors: Tencent Holdings, Contemporary Amperex Technology (CATL).

Chinese AI startup DeepSeek is nearing a funding round that could hit $15 billion, backed by giants like Tencent and CATL. This substantial capital will fuel expansion in computing infrastructure, develop advanced AI models, and grow the team ahead of a planned IPO in early 2027. It's a huge play to solidify China's position in the global AI race.

Neko Health: $700 Million Series C ($7 Billion Valuation)

Investors: Lightspeed Venture Partners (lead), O.G. Venture Partners (co-lead), Mark Zuckerberg, Priscilla Chan, OpenAI, Tim Ferriss, Maria Sharapova, will.i.am, and many more.

Co-founded by Spotify's Daniel Ek, Neko Health secured a massive Series C to expand its preventative health scanning platform into the US. The company utilizes proprietary, non-invasive full-body scanning technology to detect health issues early, pushing healthcare from reactive treatment to proactive prevention. This round brings their total funding to over $1 billion.

EliseAI: $350 Million Series F ($4 Billion Valuation)

Investors: Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) (co-lead), Bessemer Venture Partners (co-lead), Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan, Sapphire Ventures, Navitas Capital.

EliseAI, an AI-powered operations platform, doubled its valuation to $4 billion with this Series F round. The company automates back-office work for landlords and health systems, with its software now powering one in six U.S. apartments. This capital will accelerate product development and expand its engineering and sales teams, particularly into the healthcare sector.

Oratomic: $475 Million Series B ($5.4 Billion Valuation)

Investors: ARCH Venture Partners, Spark Capital, Khosla Ventures, Index Ventures, General Catalyst, Bezos Expeditions (all co-leads).

A neutral atom quantum computing company, Oratomic's Series B round brings its total funding to $775 million since March 2026. Valued now at $5.4 billion, the company is focused on building utility-scale, fault-tolerant quantum computers, making significant strides in the deep tech and AI hardware space.
