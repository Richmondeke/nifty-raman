Your weekly private capital & events briefing

Hey everyone, Richmond here from The Investor.

Another bustling week in the private capital world! It's clear that the AI wave isn't just about chatbots anymore; we're seeing some serious capital flowing into companies applying AI to the "real economy." Think factory floors, construction sites, and even defense. We also saw continued strong interest in healthtech and innovative financial platforms. It's an exciting time to be an investor, with plenty of opportunities to back the foundational technologies shaping our future.

Let's dive into the deals that caught our eye this week.

Major Deals of the Week

Armada: $230 Million Series B

Investors: Overmatch, BlackRock, 8090 Industries, Johnson Controls, NightDragon, Mitsui & Co., Singtel Innov8

Armada, an edge computing platform, raised a substantial Series B at a $2 billion pre-money valuation. They're accelerating the deployment of modular AI infrastructure, including ruggedized data centers for environments without traditional cloud connectivity, with customer bookings surging by 540% in the past year.

SendCutSend: $110 Million

Investors: Sequoia, Paradigm, Patrick Collison, John Collison

This on-demand manufacturing startup achieved a $1 billion valuation after securing $110 million. SendCutSend specializes in providing custom sheet metal and machined parts, offering a streamlined process for rapid and efficient fabrication.

Nourish: $100 Million Series C

Investors: Menlo Ventures, Thrive Capital, Index Ventures, J.P. Morgan Growth Equity Partners, Y Combinator, Atomico

Nourish, a virtual metabolic health clinic, raised significant Series C funding to expand its network of dietitians and enhance its AI-powered health platforms. They pair patients with dietitians for insurance-covered metabolic healthcare, including GLP-1 therapy support.

Moment: $78 Million Series C

Investors: Index Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Avra

An AI-native operating system for wealth and investment management, Moment closed its Series C. Its platform unifies trading, portfolio management, compliance, and reporting, now serving client firms managing over $10 trillion in combined assets.

The Deal Directory

Beyond the headlines, plenty of innovative companies secured backing this week. Here's a quick rundown of other notable rounds:

  • Ciridae: $20M Seed (Accel, a16z) for AI transformation in industrial operations.
  • Xpanner: $18M Series B Bridge (Korea Investment Partners) for construction automation robotics.
  • Arkeus: $18M Series A (QIC Ventures) for Australian defense tech and AI optical sensing.
  • Status AI: $17M Seed & Series A (General Catalyst, YC) for gamified social media.
  • Searchable: $14M Series A (Headline) for AI search optimization and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).
  • Pillar: €12M Seed (Earlybird, Base10) for AI-powered workflow software for construction contractors.
  • Stilta: $10.5M Seed (a16z, YC) for AI platform automating IP research for legal cases.
  • Ranger AI: $8.4M Seed for industrial revenue operations using agentic operating systems in manufacturing.
  • Checker: $8M Pre-Seed & Seed (Galaxy Ventures) for stablecoin liquidity infrastructure.

And a major fund launch this week: Lauxera Capital Partners announced €520 Million for their Lauxera Growth II fund, targeting growth-stage healthcare and medtech in Europe.
