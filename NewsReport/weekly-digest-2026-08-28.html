Weekly Roundup

August 28, 2026 Edition

Private capital & events briefing for HNWIs, Family Offices, and VCs

Hey there, this is Richmond from The Investor. What a week it's been in the world of private capital! If there's one thing that jumped out at me, it's the sheer force of AI. It's not just a buzzword anymore; it's the underlying tech driving massive valuations across everything from core enterprise functions like accounting, to cutting-edge robotics and even direct-to-consumer luxury fashion. We're seeing some truly eye-watering rounds, with a couple of mega-deals that clearly show investors are ready to back transformative AI that can navigate the real world, not just digital spaces. Beyond the AI boom, there's a strong current of strategic fund closes, pointing to a healthy, albeit competitive, landscape for fund managers targeting specific niches and geographies. Let's dive into the details, shall we?

Top Dealmaker of the Week

Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)

This week, Andreessen Horowitz once again proved why they remain a powerhouse in venture capital, particularly with their significant participation in Travis Kalanick's robotics venture, Atoms, which raised a staggering $1.7 billion. Their consistent backing of transformative technologies, from industrial automation to AI accounting via Rillet's $100 million Series C, underscores their commitment to foundational shifts. A16z's knack for identifying and fueling companies poised for massive impact keeps them firmly in the spotlight as a leading dealmaker.

Major Deals of the Week

Atoms: $1.7 Billion Equity Investment

Investors: Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Uber, Bain Capital, Fifth Wall, Chemistry, A*, K5 Global, Abstract, SV Angel, Alpha Square Group

Travis Kalanick's latest venture is making massive waves, securing an astounding $1.7 billion for Atoms. This robotics company is focused on industrial automation, integrating businesses like CloudKitchens and robotics firm Pronto to reshape how physical operations are managed. It's a huge bet on the future of AI-powered industrial efficiency.

Lovable: $400 Million Series C at $13.3 Billion Valuation

Investors: Menlo Ventures, EQT's Scaleup Europe Fund, Balderton Capital, Carmignac, Kaszek Ventures, LTS Growth, Tencent, World Innovation Lab, Regent, Accel, Antler, CapitalG, DST Global, Evantic Capital, HubSpot Ventures, Salesforce Ventures

Swedish unicorn Lovable continues its meteoric rise, bringing in $400 million to fuel its AI-powered software creation platform. With over 60 million projects created, their no-code solution is democratizing application development. This massive round will drive further product development and global expansion.

General Intuition: Undisclosed New Funding Talks at $6 Billion Valuation

Investors in current round talks: Valor Ventures, Point72 Ventures, Seven Seven Six

This AI startup is making headlines with discussions to raise new funding at a jaw-dropping $6 billion pre-money valuation. General Intuition is building a foundational model for generalized AI agents that can navigate space and time, with a significant pivot and expansion into robotics. Keep an eye on this deep tech player.

Instinct (Spear Street Technology): $250 Million Series B at $2.5 Billion Valuation

Investors: Index Ventures, Benchmark

Instinct, also known as Spear Street Technology, has raised a massive $250 million to fuel its AI assistant that manages personal tasks across user apps and accounts. Its valuation jumped fivefold in weeks, highlighting the immense investor appetite for sophisticated consumer AI agents capable of handling complex interactions.

Rillet: $100 Million Series C at $1 Billion Valuation

Investors: ICONIQ, Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Oak HC/FT, Bain Capital Ventures, Sequoia Global Equities, Battery Ventures, FirstMark, Scale Venture Partners, Creandum

AI accounting startup Rillet officially hit unicorn status with a $1 billion valuation after securing $100 million in Series C funding. Their AI-native ERP platform is rapidly replacing traditional accounting systems, automating financial workflows, and providing real-time insights with impressive growth numbers.
