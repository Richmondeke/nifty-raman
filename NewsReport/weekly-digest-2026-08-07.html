Weekly Roundup

August 07, 2026 Edition

Your weekly private capital & events briefing

Hey there, folks! Richmond from The Investor here.

What a week it's been! If you blinked, you might've missed the absolute tsunami of AI-related deals hitting our desks. Seriously, AI isn't just a buzzword anymore; it's the engine driving investments across every sector you can imagine, from enterprise deployment and voice interfaces to cybersecurity and financial ops.

Beyond the AI explosion, we saw some hefty bets on foundational infrastructure – whether it's powering homes, data centers, or future transportation. African fintech continues its impressive growth, showing strong potential for innovation and expansion. And let's not forget a big M&A move that shows consolidation is still very much in play. Grab a coffee, let's dive into the details.

Top Dealmaker of the Week: Insight Partners

This week, **Insight Partners** truly stood out with their strategic and consistent activity. They led multiple significant rounds, demonstrating a clear focus on the B2B SaaS and AI security space. Notably, they led the $50 million Series C for Inforcer, a London-based company helping SMBs navigate AI and security risks. Just days later, they were back, leading a $57 million Series B for Convex, a reactive backend platform for developers building in the AI era.

Their repeated, high-value investments in companies addressing critical infrastructure and security challenges in the evolving AI landscape make them our standout dealmaker. Insight Partners is clearly backing the foundational layers that will support the next generation of enterprise AI, making them a name to watch for impactful B2B plays.

Major Deals & Headlines

Base Power: $1 Billion Series D

Investors: Ribbit (lead), Addition (lead), Valor Equity Partners (lead), JPMorganChase's Strategic Investment Group (lead), Altimeter, D1 Capital Partners, Sands Capital, Coatue, Layer Global, Energy Impact Partners, Thrive Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Lightspeed, Trust Ventures, CapitalG

Austin-based Base Power is powering up the future, quite literally, with a massive $1 billion Series D. They're all about distributed energy solutions and home battery systems. This funding injection will fuel their national expansion and accelerate the deployment of their essential home battery tech, a critical piece of the climate tech puzzle.

Airtable: Acquired for $1.285 Billion

Acquirer: Bending Spoons

In a significant move in the software space, Bending Spoons has acquired Airtable for a hefty $1.285 billion. This major acquisition underscores the growing consolidation in the low-code and productivity software market, marking a substantial exit for investors and a new chapter for Airtable's popular platform.

The Boring Company: Reportedly Raising $4 Billion

Valuation: $20 Billion (Reported)

Elon Musk's tunneling venture, The Boring Company, is reportedly in advanced talks to raise a staggering $4 billion at a $20 billion valuation. This massive potential funding round signals strong investor confidence in its ambitious vision for underground transit networks, despite the complex challenges of deep tech infrastructure.

Oxide Computer: $445 Million (SEC Form D Filing)

Previous Investors: Thomas Tull's US Innovative Technology Fund, Eclipse, Riot Ventures, Jane Street

Emeryville-based Oxide Computer, founded in 2019, has secured an impressive $445 million, as disclosed in an SEC Form D filing. They provide rack-scale integrated hardware and open-source software, positioning themselves as an on-premises cloud alternative. This substantial funding highlights strong investor belief in their private, bare-metal AI infrastructure solutions.

Moove: $250 Million Series C

Investors: Mubadala Investment Company (lead), Woven Capital (co-lead), Ion Pacific (co-lead), BlueCrest Capital Management, Sona Asset Management, The Raptor Group, BlackRock, MUFG, Franklin Templeton, Uber, Left Lane, Silverbacks Holdings, Square Associates, The Latest Ventures, Endeavor Catalyst, Ontario Power Generation Pension Plan

Moove, an African mobility fintech, closed a quarter-billion dollar Series C round. They provide vehicle financing to ride-hailing and delivery drivers, and this capital will fuel their expansion into autonomous mobility and global market launches, cementing their role in the future of transport in emerging markets.
