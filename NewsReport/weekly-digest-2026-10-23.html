October 23, 2026 Edition

Weekly private capital & events briefing

Hey Investors,

Richmond from The Investor here, bringing you the lowdown on a truly electrifying week in private capital. If you thought AI was already everywhere, this past week hammered home just how deeply it's embedding itself across every sector imaginable. We saw some absolutely massive rounds, with a clear focus on the foundational tech powering the AI revolution – from cutting-edge chips to developer tools and specialized infrastructure. But it wasn't just about the silicon; legal tech, defense, and even sustainable agriculture all saw significant AI-driven investment. Buckle up, because the pace of innovation (and capital deployment) isn't slowing down!

Top Dealmaker of the Week: Sequoia Capital

This week, **Sequoia Capital** once again demonstrated its formidable presence across the venture landscape, leading or participating in several of the most impactful deals. From innovative defense technology to crucial AI infrastructure and even healthcare, their strategic investments highlight a keen eye for transformative companies at various stages. Sequoia's continued backing signals strong confidence in the long-term potential of these sectors.

They were a key investor in **Warhound's** groundbreaking $200M round for bioengineered defense dogs, led the $66M Series C for AI code platform **Blacksmith**, and also participated in **Legora's** $550M legal AI Series E and **Abby Care's** $82M healthtech Series C. A truly diversified and impactful week for the legendary firm!

Major Deals of the Week

DeepSeek: $15 Billion Funding

This Chinese AI powerhouse raised an eye-watering $15 billion, setting its sights on competing with global leaders like OpenAI. This monumental investment underscores China's ambition to dominate the AI research and large language model space, signaling a significant shift in the global AI landscape.

Investors: CATL, Tencent, Midea, Meituan, Geely, GigaDevice, Boyu Capital, CPE, Legend Capital, IDG Capital, Monolith Management, Shixiang, Stony Creek Capital

Tenstorrent: $1.5 Billion Series E ($6.2B Valuation)

Led by Jim Keller, Tenstorrent secured a massive round to scale its open-weight AI chips and challenge Nvidia's dominance. The funding ensures its independence and fuels the deployment of its Galaxy servers for AI models and licensing its advanced chip designs.

Investors: Trace Capital (lead), Innovation X (co-lead), Cadence Ventures, Eclipse, Fidelity Management & Research Company, SanDisk, T. Rowe Price, Agostinelli Group, ARK Invest, Brett Barna, Maverick Silicon, Morgan Stanley, Protagonist, Tether Investments, Troluce Capital, Virtu Financial, XTX Markets

Legora: $550 Million Series E ($8.8B Valuation)

This legal AI startup, now valued at $8.8 billion, continues its rapid ascent. Its agentic operating system (aOS) is streamlining document review, drafting, and workflow management for legal professionals globally, attracting strong investor confidence for further expansion.

Investors: Atreides Management (lead), Baron Capital, Capital Group, Fidelity Management & Research Company, Glade Brook Capital Partners, J.P. Morgan, Bessemer Venture Partners, Menlo, Accel, Benchmark, General Catalyst, ICONIQ, Insight Venture Partners, Redpoint Ventures, Salesforce Ventures, Sands Capital

Warhound: $200 Million Funding ($1.2B Valuation)

Emerging from stealth with a $1.2 billion valuation, Warhound is genetically engineering dogs for military and law enforcement, equipping them with advanced gear from Anduril. This ambitious venture aims to enhance defense capabilities through bioengineering and AI.

Investors: In-Q-Tel, Sequoia, 8VC

Town: $108 Million Series B ($1B Valuation)

Just four months post-launch, AI assistant startup Town achieved unicorn status, raising $108 million. Its "Townies" are designed to boost team productivity by connecting to digital workspaces and facilitating inter-AI collaboration.

Investors: Forerunner Ventures (co-lead), Menlo Ventures (co-lead), a16z, First Round Capital, Better Tomorrow Ventures, Precedent.vc, Scribble Ventures, Serena Ventures, Sparked Ventures, Brendan Foody, Josh Silverman, Romain Huet, Scott Belsky, Steve Bartel, Taro Fukuyama, Varun Anand

Blacksmith: $66 Million Series C ($1B Valuation)

This platform, enabling companies to effectively build and test AI code, hit unicorn valuation with a $66 million Series C. Blacksmith's focus on improving AI code quality and efficiency positions it as a key player in the evolving AI infrastructure landscape.

Investors: Sequoia (lead), Premji Invest, Peak XV Partners, GV, Y Combinator
