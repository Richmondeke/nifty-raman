Weekly Roundup

October 16, 2026 Edition

Your weekly private capital & events briefing

Hey there, folks!

Richmond from The Investor here, bringing you the lowdown on a truly electrifying week in private capital. If you blink, you might miss the next big thing, and this week was a prime example of that!

The biggest takeaway? **AI isn't just a buzzword anymore—it's building the future.** We saw massive rounds for AI infrastructure, next-gen AI models, and even AI hardware. From corporate finance to space tech and mortgage lending, AI is clearly the engine driving innovation. Fintech is still churning out big numbers, especially with companies streamlining corporate spend and revolutionizing traditional sectors like mortgages.

But it wasn't all about Silicon Valley giants. There's a strong, undeniable current pushing capital into **emerging markets, particularly Africa**. It's fantastic to see funds dedicated to nurturing talent and growth beyond the usual hubs. Grab a coffee, let's dive into the details!

Top Investor Spotlight: Sequoia Capital

This week, it's impossible not to highlight **Sequoia Capital** for their relentless pursuit of the next frontier in AI and robotics. They led the impressive $60 million Series B round for Mecka AI, a company literally teaching robots to learn from human motion. Imagine the possibilities!

Beyond robotics, Sequoia also co-led the staggering $870 million Series A for TypeSafe AI, which is redefining AI with its "calibrated decisions" model. Their continued backing of innovative, deep-tech AI solutions shows a clear strategic vision for where the future of technology is headed. Keep an eye on their portfolio; it’s a playbook for disruption.

Major Deals of the Week

Ramp: $1.85 Billion Growth Round

Investors: Dragoneer Investment Group, Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Sands Capital, Avenir Growth, Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, D1 Capital Partners, Greenoaks, ICONIQ, GIC, Coatue, D.E. Shaw

This fintech powerhouse, specializing in corporate cards and spend-management, just secured a massive $1.85 billion round, pushing its valuation to an eye-watering $60 billion. Ramp's AI-driven platform for expenses, bill pay, and procurement is clearly a game-changer for businesses.

TypeSafe AI: $870 Million Series A

Investors: Andreessen Horowitz (lead), Sequoia Capital, DCVC, Angel investors.

San Francisco's TypeSafe AI is making waves with 'Jev,' a non-text AI model delivering "calibrated decisions." This gargantuan Series A at a $7.5 billion valuation underscores its rapid adoption among Fortune 500 companies for various automation tasks.

Modal: $750 Million Series D

Investors: Accel (led), Kleiner Perkins, Menlo Ventures, General Catalyst, Redpoint, Lux Capital, Amplify Partners, Bain Capital Ventures

An AI cloud startup providing infrastructure for running and training AI models, Modal has hit a $1 billion annualized run rate. Their 'Sandboxes' for executing LLM-generated code are proving indispensable for developers.

Endeavor Catalyst: $320 Million Fund V

Investors: Over 120 Endeavor Entrepreneurs, founders of Nubank, Revolut, LinkedIn, Snowflake, Checkout.com, Globant, Careem, Reid Hoffman, Bill Ackman, Prosus, Jordan's Innovative Startups and SMEs Fund

The venture arm of global entrepreneur network Endeavor closed its oversubscribed fifth fund to co-invest in companies led by Endeavor Entrepreneurs in emerging and underserved markets. A strong commitment to global talent!

Factory: $200 Million Growth Round

Investors: Menlo Ventures, Sequoia Capital, Blackstone, NEA, Khosla Ventures, Insight Partners, Nvidia, J.P. Morgan

This AI coding startup provides an autonomous software development platform, aiming to enable enterprises to run self-improving software factories. This significant growth round shows strong belief in AI's ability to transform software creation.
