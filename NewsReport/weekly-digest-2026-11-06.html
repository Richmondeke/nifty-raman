Weekly Roundup

November 06, 2026

Weekly private capital & events briefing

Hey folks, Richmond here from The Investor! Hope you had a productive week. Looking back at the past few days, it's clear that the world of private capital is still buzzing with some seriously interesting moves. If there's one overarching theme, it's that **AI is absolutely everywhere**, driving massive M&A deals and shaping everything from home security to construction and even new forms of human-computer interaction. We're seeing big players and nimble startups alike placing huge bets on intelligent tech.

Beyond AI, there's a strong current of innovation in core infrastructure – chips, generative media, and even reimagining logistics. And it's not all about the big bucks; we're also seeing important grants and smaller raises making a real impact in healthtech and entertainment. Let's dive into the highlights!

Top Investor of the Week: Sequoia Capital

This week, **Sequoia Capital** truly stood out, demonstrating its continued commitment to cutting-edge AI innovation. The storied venture firm led the Series A round for Trajectory, a startup making AI model training accessible to non-technical experts, showcasing their belief in democratizing AI development.

Beyond that, Sequoia was also a key participant in Fal's colossal $1.65 billion Series E and F rounds, backing a generative-media infrastructure company that's pushing the boundaries of AI for image, video, and audio generation. Their strategic investments this week highlight a clear focus on foundational AI technologies and platforms designed to scale the AI revolution.

Major Deals of the Week

Thrive Holdings acquires ADT: $15.3 Billion M&A

Investors: OpenAI (backed Thrive Holdings)

OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings is making a massive play in home security by acquiring ADT. This $15.3 billion deal aims to bake advanced AI capabilities directly into home security systems, transforming the sector with smart, intelligent solutions for the modern home.

Hyperexponential acquired by Guidewire: $1.7 Billion Acquisition

Acquirer: Guidewire

Guidewire is bolstering its insurtech offerings by acquiring Hyperexponential for a hefty $1.7 billion. This strategic move will integrate AI agents into Guidewire's systems, designed to work hand-in-hand with human teams to sharpen underwriting, analysis, and pricing decisions across the insurance sector.

Fal: $1.65 Billion Series E & F

Investors: Sequoia Capital, Bessemer Venture Partners, NVIDIA, Sound Ventures, T. Rowe Price

Generative-media infrastructure company Fal just closed a massive $1.65 billion across its Series E and F rounds. They're building the platform that lets developers run and scale AI models for generating images, video, and audio, pushing the boundaries of creative AI.

FuriosaAI: $620 Million Series D

Investors: Infinitum, Jericho Capital, QUDA Capital, Cisco Investments, Worldline Capital, NeoFort Holding, South Korea's National Growth Fund, Korea Development Bank (KDB), NAVER, DSC Investment, KDB Capital, NAVER Ventures, Nextrans, Kakao Investment, Export-Import Bank of Korea, UAMCO, Hanwha Asset Management

South Korean AI semiconductor firm FuriosaAI scored $620 million in Series D funding, quadrupling its valuation to $2.9 billion. They're aiming to challenge Nvidia by building high-performance inference compute for data centers, a critical piece of the AI puzzle.

Humble Robotics: $51 Million Series A

Investors: Obvious Ventures (lead), Eclipse, Energy Impact Partners

San Francisco-based Humble Robotics raised $51 million in Series A to develop autonomous electric self-driving trucks – but here's the kicker: without drivers' cabs! They're rethinking truck design to push self-driving tech forward in the logistics sector.

Deal Directory: Early Stage & Notable Mentions

  • Trajectory: $45 Million Series A. Valued at $300M, this startup makes AI model training accessible to non-technical experts.
  • T-Unit: €50 Million in Funding. Spotify founders' new venture aims to defend European democracies with an AI-driven resilience platform.
  • Dream: $17 Million Seed. Ex-Palantir execs are building AI 'world models' for the construction industry, trained on vast building data.
  • The Subvocal Company: $1.3 Million Pre-Seed. Developing wearable tech for silent speech interaction with computers, decoding mouthed words into commands.
  • Rise of Fearless: $1 Million SAFE Financing. An African-owned gaming company expanding to reach 100,000 active players and build a globally competitive franchise.
  • Helium Health: $2.5 Million Grant. Nigerian health tech startup received Google.org funding to deploy AI tools for maternal healthcare in Nigeria.
